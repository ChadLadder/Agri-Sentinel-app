## 🎯 PART 4: Technical Interview Q&A Guide
*How to answer interview questions about Crop Disease Tracker:*

### **Q: What was your role in Crop Disease Tracker?**
> *"I co-developed the platform alongside Triyash. My focus was on building the interactive frontend components, integrating the Open-Meteo weather intelligence API for microclimate risk tracking, and designing the AI Guardrail verification pipeline that prevents LLM hallucinations."*

### **Q: How does the AI Guardrail system work?**
> *"Instead of relying solely on raw LLM outputs, we implement a 2nd-stage guardrail check that cross-references recommended chemical treatments against a deterministic ground-truth dataset (`diseases.csv`). If an unapproved or toxic chemical is outputted, the guardrail intercepts it and substitutes verified protocol guidance."*

### **Q: Why did you choose React and TypeScript?**
> *"TypeScript ensured strict type safety across our complex data models (crop species, weather metrics, protein PDB identifiers, and triage priorities). React's component-based structure allowed us to separate concerns cleanly between weather telemetry, 3D WebGL rendering, and advisor cards."*
